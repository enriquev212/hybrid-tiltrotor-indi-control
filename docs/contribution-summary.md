# Contribution Summary

This repository presents a collaborative academic Research Project. The report and defense deck list the project authors and supervisors; this public version is organized to show the technical contribution areas represented in those deliverables.

## Contribution Areas Represented Here

- Nonlinear modelling of a hybrid fixed-wing quad-tiltrotor UAV.
- Ground-anchored segmented tether modelling with slack/taut behaviour.
- Cascaded INDI controller structure for guidance and stabilization.
- WLS control allocation across rotor thrusts and tilt-angle commands.
- Simulation campaign design for the reported validation cases.
- Interpretation of tracking, cable-tension, actuator-demand and stability results.
- Preparation of the final report, defense deck and explanatory figures included in this public version.
