# References

This bibliography is taken from the final RP report and grouped by technical
topic.

## INDI and Hybrid UAV Control

- G. J. J. Ducard and M. Allenspach, "Review of designs and flight control
  techniques of hybrid and convertible VTOL UAVs," *Aerospace Science and
  Technology*, vol. 118, p. 107035, 2021.
- S. Sieberling, Q. P. Chu, and J. A. Mulder, "Robust flight control using
  incremental nonlinear dynamic inversion and angular acceleration prediction,"
  *Journal of Guidance, Control, and Dynamics*, vol. 33, no. 6, pp. 1732-1742,
  2010.
- E. J. J. Smeur, Q. Chu, and G. C. H. E. de Croon, "Adaptive incremental
  nonlinear dynamic inversion for attitude control of micro air vehicles,"
  *Journal of Guidance, Control, and Dynamics*, vol. 39, no. 3, pp. 450-461,
  2016.
- E. J. J. Smeur, G. C. H. E. de Croon, and Q. Chu, "Cascaded incremental
  nonlinear dynamic inversion for MAV disturbance rejection," *Control
  Engineering Practice*, vol. 73, pp. 79-90, 2018.
- G. Di Francesco, E. D'Amato, and M. Mattei, "Incremental nonlinear dynamic
  inversion and control allocation for a tilt rotor UAV," in *AIAA Guidance,
  Navigation, and Control Conference*, 2014. AIAA Paper 2014-0963.
- L. Zhou, J. Yang, T. Strampe, and U. Klingauf, "Incremental nonlinear dynamic
  inversion based path-following control for a hybrid quad-plane unmanned aerial
  vehicle," *International Journal of Robust and Nonlinear Control*, vol. 33,
  no. 17, pp. 10304-10327, 2023.
- M. Villanueva-Aguado and M. Bronz, "INDI control of fixed-wing tilt-rotor
  applied to tethered flight." Manuscript; file name indicates ICUAS 2026,
  2026.
- R. Revilla Bouso, "INDI control for the stabilization and guidance of the
  Heewing T1 Ranger VTOL," internship report, COVNI (ONERA) and SYSDYN (ENAC),
  Aug. 2025. Mentors: Gautier Hattenberger and Fabrice Demourant.
- E. J. J. Smeur, *Incremental Control of Hybrid Micro Air Vehicles*. PhD
  thesis, Delft University of Technology, 2018.
- H. J. Karssies and C. de Wagter, "XINCA: Extended incremental non-linear
  control allocation on the TU Delft quadplane," in *Proceedings of the 12th
  International Micro Air Vehicle Conference*, J. Martinez-Carranza, ed.,
  pp. 74-84, 2021. Article IMAV2021-9.

## Tethered Flight and Cable-Suspended Payloads

- F. Fattori and S. Cocuzza, "Tethered drones: A comprehensive review of

- P. Kotaru and K. Sreenath, "Multiple quadrotors carrying a flexible hose:

  no. 2, pp. 8832-8839, 2020.
- S. Masuda and K. Sekiyama, "Geometric control with decoupled yaw for quadrotor

- Y. Wang, G. Yu, W. Xie, W. Zhang, and C. Silvestre, "Robust cooperative

  Transportation Systems*, vol. 25, no. 9, pp. 11833-11843, 2024.
